About Me

Chris Pedraza

Game Developer

Unity & C#

3D Artist & Generalist

I'm Chris Pedraza, a game developer and 3D artist/generalist studying Games, Interactive Media and Mobile at Boise State University. I bring ideas to life through gameplay in Unity and 3D art in Blender, with a love for quirky characters, playful animation, and a little humor.

My 3D work spans modeling, texturing, rigging, and character animation. On the development side, I build games in Unity using C#, with projects including a solo-developed 2D platformer and a 3D local co-op game. Working across art and gameplay lets me connect how a game looks, moves, and feels to play.

I'm looking to contribute to game development and 3D art teams, bringing creative problem-solving, collaborative experience, and a sense of whimsy to the worlds and characters I help create.
