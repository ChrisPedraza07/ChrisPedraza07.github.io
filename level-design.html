01 / Competitive arena

Battle Arena

UnityLocal Co-op3D

My first 3D game and local co-op project. Two players start on opposing sides of an arena and compete to reach the other player's base to score points.

Defending bots attack the opposing player as they try to reach the base. I created the arena in Unity, programmed the game mechanics in C#, and made the player and bot models and animations in Blender.

Player objective
Reach the opponent's base
Challenge
Get past defending bots
My contribution
Arena creation, gameplay programming, models & animation
